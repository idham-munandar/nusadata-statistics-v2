NusaStat is an independent statistics showcase. Figures are the published values of BPS — Badan Pusat Statistik (Statistics Indonesia), the World Bank and BPJS Kesehatan, each labelled with its source and year where it appears.

Population projections are mid-year estimates; census figures are as counted. HDI is the BPS IPM series. Nothing on this page is fetched — the whole portal runs from its own files.
